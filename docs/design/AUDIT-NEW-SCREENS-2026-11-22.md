## Checks that passed

- No full-colour filled buttons. Measured computed styles: `.btn-primary` `rgba(255,106,43,0.10)` / `rgba(255,138,61,0.08)`, `.engine-chip` `rgba(...,0.1)`, selected `.seg-btn` surface colours, `.btn-secondary` a surface gradient. Tint tokens are `--a06/--a10/--a16` (cinder `packs/themes/cinder/theme.css:20-21`, lumen `:15-16`, neo-roma `:27-28`) and the rules consuming them are `web/src/base.css:125,194-195,248,578`. Zero filled-button hits across all 288 states.
- Complete Polish vocabulary: `web/src/i18n.ts` has 423 keys in `en` (starts line 7) and 423 in `pl` (starts line 432); no missing or extra keys. `t()` falls back to English only when a key is absent (`:934-935`), which never happened. 21 values are intentionally identical (CaesarOS, Latin names, `Gateway`, `Offline`, `Model`, `Terminal`, `Start`, `English`, `Polski`, etc.). No English dictionary string was found in any Polish page text. `English`/`Polski` in `web/src/pages/Start.tsx:110-111` are intentional language names, not a missing translation.
- No touch targets below 44 px on any 390x844 state, including the `/start` step dots (`web/src/base.css:566-569`) and the phone bar. The earlier 43/41 px top-bar regression is gone.
- Safe-area top is now applied (`web/src/base.css:275` uses `env(safe-area-inset-top)` in the topbar padding and height), and the phone content inset includes the bar and bottom safe area (`:279`).
- No horizontal overflow at 1440x900, and no console/page errors in 286 of 288 states.
- No clipped text on desktop; all 150 clipped-text cases are the phone title described in N-02. Elements inside intentionally horizontal scrollers (chat session strip, step dots) were excluded from the document-overflow count.

- Screen-reader labels, focus order, keyboard traps, contrast ratios and reduced-motion behaviour.
- Interaction beyond navigation and step selection: sending chat messages, saving settings, downloading models, backup creation or module toggles were not exercised. The session with title `??` seen in chat is data already stored on port 4410, not a placeholder bug.
- The transient errors in N-04 could not be reproduced, so their root cause and line numbers remain unverified.
- Landscape, 320 px width, tablets, other text scales and locales beyond en/pl.
